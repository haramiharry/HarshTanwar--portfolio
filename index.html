How I Work

01 · Discovery
Assess Before Acting
Every project starts with thorough discovery. I run stakeholder workshops, map current state processes, identify risks and constraints, and define clear success criteria before any implementation begins. Assumptions get documented. Surprises get minimized.
02 · Planning
Plans That Get Used
I build project plans that are actually used: risk registers that get updated, RAID logs that drive decisions, and status reports that tell the truth about progress. Planning is a living activity, not a one-time document.
03 · Stakeholders
Alignment at Every Level
I manage stakeholders from C-suite to end user. Executive steering committees get clear business-impact reporting. Operations teams get practical guidance. End users get change management that makes adoption stick.
04 · Delivery
Go-Live Accountability
I own go-live readiness. UAT is structured, exit criteria are defined, and post-implementation reviews confirm that outcomes are real and measurable. No project is complete until KPIs are confirmed against baseline.
05 · Methodology
Hybrid by Default
PMP certified with practical experience in agile, waterfall, and hybrid approaches. I adapt methodology to the project, not the other way around. What matters is delivery, not dogma.
06 · Improvement
Every Project Better
I run retrospectives, capture lessons learned, and apply them systematically. Continuous improvement is built into every engagement through structured reflection and documented outcomes.
